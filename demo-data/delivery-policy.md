# Eastern Industrial Supply Co., Ltd.

## Delivery Policy DL-02

Effective date: 15 August 2026  
Dispatch warehouse: RYG-A

### Rayong Industrial Zone

- Orders with a net product value of 100,000 THB or more receive free standard delivery.
- Orders confirmed before 15:00 are allocated stock on the same business day.
- Standard delivery lead time is 3 business days when stock is available at warehouse RYG-A.
- Urgent next-day delivery requires Logistics Manager confirmation and may incur a surcharge.
- Delivery includes transport to the customer receiving area. Installation and commissioning are excluded.

### Other Destinations

Delivery cost and lead time must be calculated by the logistics team according to weight, vehicle type, and destination.
