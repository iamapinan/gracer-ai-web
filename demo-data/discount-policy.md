# Eastern Industrial Supply Co., Ltd.

## Sales Discount Policy SD-04

Effective date: 1 July 2026  
Approver: Commercial Director

### Standard Project Discount

| Order value before VAT | Maximum automatic discount |
| --- | ---: |
| Below 100,000 THB | 0% |
| 100,000-499,999 THB | 3% |
| 500,000-899,999 THB | 5% |
| 900,000 THB and above | 7% |

### Rules

1. Discount is calculated from the current standard price list.
2. The 7% tier may be applied automatically when the gross product value is at least 900,000 THB.
3. Discounts above the published tier require Commercial Director approval.
4. Delivery fees and VAT are excluded from the discount calculation.
5. Promotion discounts cannot be combined with the project discount.
