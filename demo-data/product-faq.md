# XP Series Product FAQ

## Can the XP-200 pump corrosive chemicals?

The standard XP-200 is intended for process water and general industrial liquids compatible with stainless steel 316. Concentrated chemicals require an engineering review.

## Is installation included?

No. Standard quotations include the pump and delivery only. Installation and commissioning must be quoted separately.

## What is the warranty period?

The standard warranty is 18 months after delivery, subject to correct installation and operation.

## Is the XP-200 available for immediate delivery?

Availability must be confirmed against the latest warehouse stock snapshot before issuing a quotation.
